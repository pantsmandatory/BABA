How to read this. 3000K configurations at 480V that a state DOT flags for Buy America. Caltrans (California) marks each row with an explicit BABA category and requires 3000K. The TxDOT rows are TxDOT's 4000K Compliant v5 listings with the 3000K option swapped in, so they are not listed as written; confirm Buy America certification with the manufacturer.

"Compliant v5" = manufacturer certified through TxDOT's Gold Star Program for projects federally obligated on or after Oct 1, 2026 (Phase 2).
