How to read this. Each card is a luminaire configuration that a state DOT lists as Compliant v5, meaning the manufacturer certified it for federal-aid projects obligated on or after Oct 1, 2026 (US final assembly and more than 55% domestic component cost). DOT lists usually qualify one color temperature; the catalog number shown here swaps in the 3000K / 70 CRI option of the same listed configuration. None of these 3000K catalog numbers is listed by TxDOT as written (TxDOT qualified the 4000K versions), so ask the manufacturer to confirm the 3000K build carries the same Buy America certification before you specify it.
  • TX – TxDOT Material Producer List – Roadway Illumination and Electrical Supplies (September 1, 2026)
    "Compliant v5" = manufacturer certified through TxDOT's Gold Star Program for projects federally obligated on or after Oct 1, 2026 (Phase 2). "Compliant v4" covers Oct 1, 2025 – Sept 30, 2026 only and is not shown here.
