Flagged listings that don't show 480V or a color temperature

These DOT entries are flagged for Buy America, but the listing itself does not print a 480V voltage code (it shows a placeholder such as xx*, x or X, or no catalog number at all) or a color temperature. The product family offers a 480V option, but that is not evidence the listed configuration is 480V; for example, Caltrans spells out its 480V variants separately as "(480 VAC)". Confirm the exact configuration with the manufacturer and the DOT.
