How to read this. Every 480V configuration that a state DOT list prints, with this exact model number, as Phase 2 compliant (US final assembly and more than 55% domestic component cost, for projects obligated on or after Oct 1, 2026), at any color temperature. TxDOT's Compliant v5, Alabama's Y2 code and Caltrans's "55% or higher cost and final assembly both in US" all mean Phase 2. The CCT column shows the color temperature the listing prints.
Phase 2 · 55% DOT says US final assembly and more than 55% domestic cost (projects obligated on/after Oct 1, 2026). Only these listings are shown.
  • AL – ALDOT MSDSAR List IV-6: Roadway Lighting (Revision November 5, 2026). BABA column: Y2 = manufactured product, BABA compliant; N2 = final assembly only, fails the Oct 1, 2026 55% rule; N1 = fails the Oct 1, 2025 final-assembly rule. Y2 is therefore treated as Phase 2.
  • CA – Caltrans Authorized Materials List – LED Luminaires (Last edit date: 11/8/2026). "BABA Act Information" column: "Manufactured Product, 55% or higher cost and final assembly both in US" (Phase 2) or "only final assembly (manufacturing) in US" (Phase 1). Caltrans criteria require nominal 3000K.
  • TX – TxDOT Material Producer List – Roadway Illumination and Electrical Supplies (September 1, 2026). "Compliant v5" = manufacturer certified through TxDOT's Gold Star Program for projects federally obligated on or after Oct 1, 2026 (Phase 2). "Compliant v4" covers Oct 1, 2025 – Sept 30, 2026 only and is not shown here.
