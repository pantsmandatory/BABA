Which rule applies.
  • Fiber optic cable and optical fiber are construction materials, not manufactured products (2 CFR 184.3). Every manufacturing step must be in the US: preform fabrication and draw for the fiber, then ribboning, buffering, stranding and jacketing for the cable (2 CFR 184.6(a)(4)–(5)). There is no 55% cost test. 2 CFR 184 · FHWA BABA Q&A
  • Splice housings, cassettes and connectorized jumpers are manufactured products: US manufacture for projects obligated on or after Oct 1, 2025, plus more than 55% domestic component cost from Oct 1, 2026. Steel cabinets/enclosures must meet the iron and steel rule. 23 CFR 635.410
  • Corning badge: 'Family-level' means only Corning's company-wide BABAA statement covers it (that page blocks automated access, so its wording here comes from search results). 'Not stated' means Corning publishes no BABA claim for that part. No product here has a part-specific Corning BABA statement.

Splice housings for traffic / ITS cabinets

Rack- and wall-mount housings, splice trays and cassettes that fit inside a signal or ITS field cabinet. These are manufactured products. Corning's catalog marks none of them BABA; FDOT lists them as 'BABA Eligible' for named projects only. Corning's BEAD-BABA hardware (2178 closures, FTH NIDs, GEN III cabinets) is outside-plant broadband gear, not cabinet hardware.

Orange multimode cable (OM1 / OM2)

Orange is the TIA-598 color for OM1 and OM2; OM3/OM4 are aqua. Only Corning's indoor MIC family ships orange; FREEDM and ALTOS multimode cables are black. Standard Corning jumpers/pigtails checked in eCatalog are flagged 'BEAD/BABA – Not Compliant', so none are listed here.
