What counts as compliant here. Under FHWA's manufactured-products rule (23 CFR 635.410), a luminaire must have final assembly in the United States and, for projects obligated on or after Oct 1, 2026, domestic components costing more than 55% of all component cost. An entry appears in the main table only when it is on at least one state DOT approved list and both tests are documented by the DOT or by the manufacturer for that product family. This index is a research aid, not a certification: always obtain the manufacturer's project-specific Buy America certification and confirm with the DOT.
  • Oct 1, 2025 – Federal-aid projects obligated on or after this date: manufactured products must have final assembly in the United States.
  • Oct 1, 2026 – Projects obligated on or after this date: final assembly in the US AND domestic components > 55% of total component cost (iron/steel components count toward the 55%).
Predominantly iron/steel cabinets or enclosures must meet FHWA iron and steel rules. De minimis waiver: non-compliant products totaling no more than the lesser of $1 million or 5% of total applicable project costs. Confirm current rule status with FHWA before relying on it. [1] · [2]
